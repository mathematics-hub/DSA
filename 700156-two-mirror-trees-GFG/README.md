# [Two Mirror Trees](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/two-mirror-trees/1)
## Easy
Given two binary trees, find if the two trees are mirror images of each other. Two binary trees are considered mirrors if:

 Their root nodes have the same value. 
 The left subtree of one tree is the mirror of the right subtree of the other tree. 
 The right subtree of one tree is the mirror of the left subtree of the other tree.&nbsp; &nbsp;

Examples:
Input:
Output: trueExplanation: Given pairs of binary tree are mirror of each other.

Input:
Output: falseExplanation:  Given pairs of binary tree are not mirror of each other.
